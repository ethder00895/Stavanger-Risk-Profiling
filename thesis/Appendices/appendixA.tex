\chapter{Sample Appendix}

Typically you should have at least one Appendix. This is where you would detail where the outputs of your project are located on a platform like GitHub or OneDrive. Discuss what is needed and the format of this appendix with your Supervisor.

Other appendices can be used for containing additional plots, data, copies of questionnaires or other material relevant to your work but that does not fit in the main body. All appendices must be referred to in the text.  Keep an eye on the length, as a rule these should \textbf{not} exceed 25-30\% of the body text. Longer material. or material intended to be machine processed (e.g. data tables, source code) should be placed in an electronic appendix.
